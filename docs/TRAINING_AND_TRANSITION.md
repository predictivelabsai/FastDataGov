# Training and transition

## Role-based enablement

| Audience | Practical outcomes | Suggested session |
|---|---|---:|
| Consumers/analysts | Search, assess trust/lineage/quality, choose a product, request access | 45 min |
| Stewards | Enrich/version metadata, manage glossary/lineage/rules and resolve work | 90 min lab |
| Owners | Review evidence, decide access/certification, attest accountability | 60 min lab |
| Engineers/admins | Configure identity/adapters, operate jobs, diagnose and recover | 2 hour lab |
| Governance leads | Configure operating controls and interpret outcome measures | 60 min |

Use the deterministic demo before tenant access, then repeat representative journeys with pilot data. Training evidence should include attendance, exercises completed, unanswered questions and a named follow-up owner.

## Handover package

- Approved architecture/security/deployment configuration.
- Connection inventory and secret-reference ownership (never secret values).
- Domain, workflow, role/alias and notification configuration export.
- Reconciliation results, accepted limitations and open backlog.
- Operations, incident, backup/restore, upgrade and rollback evidence.
- Named product owner, service owner, platform operator and support escalation.

## Hypercare

Use a default two-week period after pilot release: daily health review in week one, then twice weekly; triage critical access/security/data-trust issues immediately; maintain a decision log; close only after steady job health, known ownership and accepted residual gaps. Duration is locally agreed.

## Exit criteria

Operations can deploy, restore, rotate secrets, test connections, reconcile syncs and resolve/retry jobs without implementation-team access. Governance users can complete all five workflows. Documentation and support ownership are accepted, and temporary delivery credentials are revoked.
